Why Summit

Why This Costs More
Than a Kit From the Store

A $600 DIY kit and a professional coating are not the same product installed by different people. They are different systems, and the difference is almost entirely in the preparation.

Full Mechanical Grind — Not an Acid Etch

We diamond-grind every slab down to a bare, open profile with HEPA dust collection, so the coating bonds into the concrete instead of sitting on top of it. Skipping this step is the single most common reason garage floors peel — and it is the step every DIY kit skips.

Broadcast to Refusal, Sealed With Polyaspartic

A full vinyl flake broadcast — flake until the floor will not take any more, not a light scatter — locked in under a UV-stable polyaspartic topcoat that will not amber or chalk under high-altitude sun.

Built for 9,000 Feet, Not for Denver

Freeze-thaw cycling, winter road salt, ski edges, studded tires, and a garage that swings 60 degrees in a day. We choose systems for what actually happens in a mountain garage, because that is the only kind we install.

Lifetime Warranty From a Local Crew

Lifetime warranty on the coating against delamination and peeling, plus a moisture vapor barrier option carrying its own moisture-related warranty. We live and work here — if a floor ever needs attention, you are calling the crew that installed it.

Straight Answer

What a Garage Floor
Actually Costs

Most Colorado mountain-town garage floors range from approximately $3,000 to $7,000+. Every garage is different — the condition of the concrete matters more than the square footage — so we price yours in person rather than guessing from a number over the phone.

What actually moves the price

Concrete condition

Cracks, spalling, pitting, and old failing coatings all need repair or removal before anything goes down. Two garages the same size can price very differently because of this, and it is why we will not quote a floor we have not walked.

Moisture in the slab

Some mountain slabs push moisture up from below, which will lift a coating over time. Where we find it, we install a moisture vapor barrier (MVB) system that carries its own moisture-related warranty. It adds cost, and it is the difference between a floor that lasts and one that peels.

System and topcoat

Every floor we install is fully mechanically ground, base coated, broadcast to refusal with vinyl flake, and sealed with a UV-stable polyaspartic topcoat. Custom flake blends and heavier build coats sit at the upper end of the range.

A minimum project price applies to smaller floors — mobilising a grinder, a HEPA vacuum, and a two-person crew to a mountain town costs the same no matter how small the space is.

We will inspect your garage and give you an exact, written price at your free in-person estimate — not a range, and not a guess over the phone.
